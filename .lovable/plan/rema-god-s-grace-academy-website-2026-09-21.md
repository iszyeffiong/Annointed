# Annointed comprehensive high school Website

## Goal
Build a polished, mobile-first seven-page school website that helps families understand the school and reach admissions within three clicks.

## Pages and shared structure
- Create Home, About, Academics, Admissions, Gallery, News & Events, and Contact pages with distinct URLs and page-specific metadata.
- Add a persistent responsive header, animated mobile menu, admissions call-to-action, and a shared footer with the supplied Uyo address and Facebook link.
- Keep phone, email, WhatsApp, opening hours, leadership names, fees, dates, and results clearly presented as replaceable placeholders rather than inventing official details.

## Visual direction
- Use a deep forest/navy foundation, warm amber-gold accents, and paper-white surfaces inspired by academic regalia and West African textile rhythm.
- Pair an expressive editorial serif with a clean sans-serif, loaded efficiently from the page head.
- Create lightweight illustrated motifs and abstract campus-life compositions in CSS rather than using photographs of children.
- Establish all colors, typography, shadows, spacing, and motion as reusable design tokens.

## Interactions
- Add a branded Home-page intro lasting about 1.6 seconds, capped below three seconds and shown once per browser session.
- Add one subtle section-reveal treatment, tactile hover states, visible keyboard focus, and a pointer-responsive hero spotlight.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep navigation fully keyboard accessible.
- Build client-side admissions and contact forms with validation and a clear success state; label them as prototype submissions that are not yet delivered externally.

## Content
- Write warm, faith-informed placeholder mission, vision, values, leadership, curriculum, programmes, clubs, testimonials, announcements, and events.
- Cover Creche/Nursery, Primary, and Secondary levels clearly.
- Include a lightweight illustrated gallery, admissions checklist and process, contact details placeholders, map placeholder, and Facebook link.

## Verification
- Check all seven pages at desktop and mobile widths.
- Exercise the mobile menu, internal navigation, both forms, one-session intro behavior, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Confirm there are no current preview errors and every page has unique social and search metadata.
